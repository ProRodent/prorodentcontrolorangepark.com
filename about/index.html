What we do.

We handle mouse and Norway rat problems for homes and businesses in Orange Park and the nearby towns listed on our areas page. Every visit follows the same order: inspect the property to find where activity is actually coming from, treat with trapping or exclusion matched to what was found, and seal the entry points so the problem does not simply return.

That order matters more than any single product or technique. A trap set without knowing the travel route rarely works, and a seal job done without finding every gap just leaves another one open.

How we explain a job.

You hear the plan and the price before any work starts, based on what the inspection actually found, not a generic estimate given over the phone. If damage from chewed wiring, insulation or ducts is found, it gets documented and priced as its own line item rather than folded quietly into a larger total.

Availability.

The line is answered 24 hours a day, and same-day visits are offered where the schedule allows. Whether the call comes in about a scratching sound at midnight or a routine inspection scheduled a week out, the same inspect, treat, seal approach applies.

Why we inspect before anything else.

A technician who skips the inspection and goes straight to setting traps or quoting a price is guessing. Every house, and every yard, has its own combination of age, construction style and landscaping that shapes where rodents actually get in. We would rather spend the time up front finding the real entry points than return three times because the first guess missed the actual gap.

This matters as much for a quick mouse call as it does for a larger commercial account. The scale of the job changes; the order of operations, inspect, treat, seal, follow up, does not.

Documenting damage honestly.

When an inspection or cleanup turns up chewed insulation, wiring or ductwork, we document it with photos and price it as its own line item rather than folding it quietly into a larger total. Homeowners deserve to know exactly what was found and why it costs what it costs, not a vague lump sum they have to take on faith.

If you are weighing whether to call us or try a store-bought trap first, a short phone conversation can help you decide. We would rather talk through what you are seeing for a few minutes than have you spend weeks on a fix that was never going to address the actual entry point.
