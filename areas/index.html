Coverage overview

Orange Park itself carries two coverage ZIP codes, 32065 and 32073. Several of the towns listed below are unincorporated communities, Fleming Island, Middleburg and Saint Johns, each governed directly by its county with no separate town government of its own. We describe each one honestly rather than treating every nearby place the same way.

Green Cove Springs and Penney Farms are genuinely incorporated municipalities in Clay County, each with its own local government. Saint Johns sits across the county line in St. Johns County rather than Clay County, which we flag honestly rather than blur.

Jacksonville is the one large exception on this list, a consolidated city-county government, merged with Duval County in 1968. Of its many ZIP codes, only the 13 that fall within about 15 miles of Orange Park are covered here; ZIP codes further into the city are outside our confirmed coverage range.

Questions about our coverage area

How did you decide which towns to include?

Each town had to appear in our coverage data and be confirmed within about 15 miles of Orange Park by independent ZIP-code distance. Towns that only passed one check were left off.

Why isn't every Jacksonville ZIP code listed?

Jacksonville is a large consolidated city-county with ZIP codes spanning a wide area. Only the 13 that fall within our confirmed 15-mile range from Orange Park are listed; the rest are outside our coverage.

Do you serve towns outside Clay County?

Yes. Saint Johns falls in St. Johns County rather than Clay County, and we cover it the same way, verified by coverage data and distance.
