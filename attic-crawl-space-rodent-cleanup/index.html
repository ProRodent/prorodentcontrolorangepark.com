Why attics and crawl spaces accumulate activity unnoticed.

Attics and crawl spaces in Orange Park homes often go months or years without anyone actually looking inside them, which is exactly the kind of undisturbed space mice and Norway rats prefer. North Florida's humidity means droppings, urine staining and damaged insulation can sit in an unused attic for a long time before anyone notices a smell or a sound.

Crawl spaces under older raised-floor construction see this just as often as attics, since the same lack of regular foot traffic applies underneath the house as it does above the ceiling.

What cleanup actually involves.

Droppings and nesting material are not simply swept or vacuumed, since disturbing dried droppings can spread contamination into the air. The space is cleaned using methods that keep material contained, bagged and removed properly, following the same wetting-before-disturbing approach public health guidance recommends for rodent droppings.

  • Droppings and nesting material removed without dry sweeping or vacuuming.
  • Damaged or contaminated insulation identified and documented.
  • The space sanitized after removal, not just cleared of visible debris.
Cleaning up after rodents is not the same as cleaning up after dust.

Cleanup only after the entry point is sealed.

Cleaning an attic or crawl space before rodent exclusion and entry point sealing closes the gap means doing the same work twice, since new animals can simply use the same opening. We confirm the entry points are closed first, then clean, so the work actually stays done.

Insulation and duct damage.

If chewed or urine-damaged insulation or ductwork turns up during cleanup, it gets documented honestly as its own line item under rodent damage repair rather than folded quietly into the cleanup cost. You see exactly what was found and why it is priced the way it is before any repair work starts.

Before we arrive

  • Keep children and pets away from any area with droppings, a nest, or a suspected entry point.
  • Do not sweep or vacuum dry droppings; that can spread contamination into the air.
  • Do not set out your own loose bait or poison before the visit.
  • Note when and where you have seen or heard activity so the technician can start there.

Questions about attic and crawl space rodent cleanup

Can I clean up droppings myself?

It is not recommended to sweep or vacuum dry droppings, since that can spread contamination into the air. A proper cleanup contains and removes material without disturbing it that way.

Do you clean before or after sealing entry points?

After. Cleaning before the gap is closed risks new activity undoing the work, so sealing comes first.

What if insulation is damaged?

It gets documented and priced separately under rodent damage repair, so you know exactly what was found and what it costs to fix.
