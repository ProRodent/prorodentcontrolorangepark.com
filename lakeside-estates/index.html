A shoreline neighborhood with its own pressure.

Lakeside Estates is a lake-oriented neighborhood, and for any home here that backs onto or sits close to the water, that proximity shapes the rodent pressure the property deals with. Norway rats do well around lake edges with landscaped plantings, since the combination of water, cover and nearby food sources, whether from residential compost, bird feeders or food left out for pets, gives them most of what they need within a short distance.

Homes directly backing onto the water tend to see more outdoor burrow activity along the rear fence line or shoreline vegetation than homes set back on an interior street in the same neighborhood, simply because of the extra cover the water's edge provides.

What draws activity near the lake.

  • Shoreline landscaping and mature plantings checked for burrow activity.

Sealing and yard work together.

For homes near the water, backyard and outdoor rodent control and standard exclusion work usually go together, since outdoor activity along the shoreline is often what eventually pushes toward the house itself once the weather turns cooler or drier. Before we arrive

  • Keep children and pets away from any area with droppings, a nest, or a suspected entry point.
  • Do not sweep or vacuum dry droppings; that can spread contamination into the air.
  • Do not set out your own loose bait or poison before the visit.

Questions about Lakeside Estates

Does living on Doctors Lake mean more rodent activity?

Homes directly backing onto the lake do tend to see more outdoor burrow activity than homes on interior streets, mainly from Norway rats using the shoreline cover.

Do bird feeders near the water attract rodents?

They can, along with compost and pet food left outdoors. We check all of these during a yard inspection in this neighborhood.

Should I be more concerned in a particular season?

Activity often increases as the weather cools and rodents look for cover and warmth, which is when outdoor activity near the lake is most likely to move toward nearby homes.
